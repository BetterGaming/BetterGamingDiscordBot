## 1. Information the Bot may process

Depending on which features are enabled and how users interact with the Bot, SwitchBot may access or process:

- **Discord identifiers:** user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, message IDs, and role IDs when required for commands, permissions, configuration, or moderation.
- **Command inputs and messages:** commands, their arguments, and message content that the Bot is permitted to access and needs to process for requested features, content/link filtering, or moderation.
- **Server settings:** configuration information needed to support features such as custom commands and link restrictions.
- **Moderation and diagnostic information:** relevant events, errors, timestamps, and technical logs needed to operate, troubleshoot, or protect the Bot.

The exact information processed depends on Discord permissions, enabled features, and server configuration. The Bot does not need access to every message for every feature.

## 2. Why information is used

Information is used only as needed to operate requested commands and features; apply configured moderation or link rules; provide news and AI responses; maintain server settings; troubleshoot problems; prevent abuse; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 3. AI-assisted features and third parties

If an AI-assisted feature is used, the content submitted to that feature, and potentially relevant conversation context, **may be transmitted to the configured AI service provider (for example, OpenAI)** to generate a response. Users should not submit passwords, access tokens, sensitive personal information, or confidential material to AI features.

The Bot also interacts with **Discord** and may use third-party **hosting providers** and **news/feed sources** to deliver its functionality. These parties may process information according to their own applicable terms and privacy notices. The operator should identify actual providers if required by applicable law.

The operator does not authorize selling Discord user data or using it for targeted advertising.

## 4. Storage and retention

Some data may be processed only while a request is handled; other information, such as server settings, moderation records, or technical logs, may be retained where needed to provide features, diagnose incidents, or comply with legal obligations.

Information should be kept **only for as long as necessary** for these purposes and then deleted or de-identified, unless retention is required by law. **The operator must verify actual retention periods and deletion mechanisms before publishing this policy.**
