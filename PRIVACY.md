# SwitchBot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 15 October 2026  
**Last updated:** 6 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **SwitchBot** (the “Bot”) handles information when you interact with it on Discord. SwitchBot provides Discord server utilities, which may include moderation, custom commands, link restrictions, Nintendo/homebrew news, and AI-assisted responses.

**Operator:** Better Gaming  
**Privacy contact:** bettercreative.media@outlook.com

## 1. Information the Bot may process

Depending on which features are enabled and how users interact with the Bot, SwitchBot may access or process:

- **Discord identifiers:** user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, message IDs, and role IDs when required for commands, permissions, configuration, or moderation.
- **Command inputs and messages:** commands, their arguments, and message content that the Bot is permitted to access and needs to process for requested features, content/link filtering, or moderation.
- **Server settings:** configuration information needed to support features such as custom commands and link restrictions.
- **Moderation and diagnostic information:** relevant events, errors, timestamps, and technical logs needed to operate, troubleshoot, or protect the Bot.

The exact information processed depends on Discord permissions, enabled features, and server configuration. The Bot does not need access to every message for every feature.

## 2. Why information is used

Information is used only as needed to operate requested commands and features; apply configured moderation or link rules; provide news and AI responses; maintain server settings; troubleshoot problems; prevent abuse; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 3. AI-assisted features and third parties

If an AI-assisted feature is used, the content submitted to that feature, and potentially relevant conversation context, **may be transmitted to the configured AI service provider (for example, OpenAI)** to generate a response. Users should not submit passwords, access tokens, sensitive personal information, or confidential material to AI features.

The Bot also interacts with **Discord** and may use third-party **hosting providers** and **news/feed sources** to deliver its functionality. These parties may process information according to their own applicable terms and privacy notices. SwitchBot currently uses OpenAI for AI-assisted responses and is hosted on Bot-Hosting.net.

The operator does not authorize selling Discord user data or using it for targeted advertising.

## 4. Storage and retention

Some data may be processed only while a request is handled; other information, such as server settings, moderation records, or technical logs, may be retained where needed to provide features, diagnose incidents, or comply with legal obligations.

AI support sessions and solved support cases are stored in an encrypted database on the Bot's host. Administrators can also collect solved questions from a channel as unverified candidates, which staff review and can discard. **This data is not deleted automatically after a set period, so it may be kept for longer than 30 days.** It is removed when a deletion request is handled (see section 5).

## 5. Deletion requests and privacy choices

Users can ask about their data, request correction where applicable, or request deletion by contacting **bettercreative.media@outlook.com** and providing sufficient details to identify the relevant Discord account or data. Server administrators can remove the Bot and change its permissions or settings at any time.

The operator will evaluate and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law and Discord's Developer Terms. Removing the Bot from a server does not automatically prove that previously stored records have been erased; data no longer needed should be deleted as required.

## 6. Security

The operator takes reasonable steps to protect Bot credentials and information processed by the Bot, including limiting access to hosting accounts and application secrets and encrypting the AI support database at rest. However, no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Children's privacy

The Bot is intended for use on Discord by people who meet Discord's minimum age requirements. It is not designed to collect personal information from children below the age permitted to use Discord.

## 8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the Bot's features, practices, or legal requirements. The current version and its effective date will be published at this URL.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact **bettercreative.media@outlook.com**.
